# Audience — EMS

## Primary: the outbound agency
Runs cold campaigns for 5–40 clients. Today they pay Instantly or Smartlead a
platform fee *plus* $3–5 per mailbox per month across 100–300 mailboxes, and they
still cannot answer "why did placement drop last Tuesday." They buy infrastructure
they can point at, and a compliance artifact their clients' legal teams accept.

## Secondary: the in-house SDR team
5–30 reps at a B2B company. Cares about reply rate and meetings booked, not about
MTAs — but their security and legal review is what kills most vendor evaluations.
Our per-send lawful-basis record is what gets us through it.

## Tertiary: the founder-led seller
One to three mailboxes, high personalisation, low volume. Cheap for us to serve on
a shared warmed pool and a good top-of-funnel, but never a dedicated IP.

## What they actually ask for, in order
1. "Will it land in the inbox?" — answered with seed placement, not adjectives.
2. "How fast can I start?" — answered honestly: ~14 days of warmup, with a countdown.
3. "Is this legal where I'm sending?" — answered by the jurisdiction map.
4. "What happens when it breaks?" — answered by the message journey view.

## Who we decline
Anyone who wants to send to a purchased list with no stated provenance, anyone in
the prohibited content categories, and anyone we cannot identify. Not a moral
posture — one abusive customer damages every other customer on our IPs.
