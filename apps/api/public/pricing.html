Questions

Pricing FAQ

Are you charging yet?

No. Stripe checkout is not live. You can create an account and send on Free. The dollar amounts on this page are the published list prices for when billing turns on.

Is the free tier a trial?

No. Free is a permanent tier: 3,000 emails/mo, 1 domain, the full Resend-compatible API, logs, signed webhooks, and DKIM/DMARC automation. When you hit the cap, further sends are rejected until next month or a plan change. No card, no expiry.

How will overage work?

Each paid plan includes a monthly volume; past that, $0.70/1k on Pro, $0.55/1k on Scale, $0.30/1k wholesale on White-label. Those rates are not invoiced today. Plan send caps are already enforced in the API.

Do you sell dedicated IPs or SSO?

Not yet. Dedicated IP pools, managed warmup, and SSO are on the roadmap. We do not list them as included features.

I'm on another email API today — how hard is switching?

Your existing Resend client works with a single base-URL change (RESEND_BASE_URL=https://api.electronicmailservices.com). Start on Free to validate, then raise the cap when billing is live.
